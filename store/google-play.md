# Google Play – store listing

Texts to paste into Play Console (Grow > Store presence > Main store listing). The App Store
texts live in `store.config.json` and are uploaded with `npx eas-cli metadata:push`.

Limits: app name 30, short description 80, full description 4000 characters.

```
Dishdeck to Twoja talia przepisów: zapisujesz ulubione dania, układasz z nich plan na tydzień, a lista zakupów tworzy się sama.

PRZEPISY
• Przepisy w grupach: śniadania, obiady, kolacje albo dowolne własne
• Zdjęcie do każdego przepisu, żeby od razu było wiadomo, co to za danie

JADŁOSPIS OD DIETETYKA

LISTA ZAKUPÓW
• Generowana z zaznaczonych posiłków planu
• Składniki zsumowane i pogrupowane według działów sklepu
• Ilości możesz poprawić ręcznie, a kupione produkty odhaczasz

PRYWATNOŚĆ
Dishdeck działa w całości offline. Nie wymaga konta, nie ma reklam i nie wysyła żadnych danych – wszystko zostaje na Twoim telefonie.

Aplikacja dostępna po polsku i angielsku.
```

## English (en-US)

**App name**

```
Dishdeck: Meal Plan & Grocery
```

**Short description**

```
Meal plans from your dietitian, a weekly planner and a grocery list. Offline.
```

**Full description**

```
Dishdeck is your deck of recipes: save the dishes you love, lay them out into a weekly plan, and let the shopping list write itself.

RECIPES
• Recipes in groups: breakfasts, lunches, dinners or your own
• A photo for every recipe, so you know the dish at a glance
• Star ratings – remember what you liked most
• Search and filter by group

YOUR DIETITIAN'S MEAL PLAN
• Import a meal plan from a Word file (.doc) – recipes, ingredients and amounts come straight in
• Group recipes by meal plan to pick mostly from the newest one
• Built-in substitutes (e.g. groats instead of rice, turkey instead of chicken) with recalculated amounts – swap an ingredient in one planned meal while the recipe stays unchanged

WEEKLY PLAN
• Your own meals of the day: 1 to 8, with your names and order
• Servings with multipliers, including halves (1.5×, 2.5×)
• Draw random meals for a day or a week – never the same dish two days in a row

SHOPPING LIST
• Generated from the meals you pick in the plan
• Ingredients added up and grouped by store aisle
• Adjust amounts by hand and tick items off as you shop

PRIVACY
Dishdeck works fully offline. No account, no ads and no data sent anywhere – everything stays on your phone.

Available in English and Polish.
```
